Programs

Programs that strengthen learning, collaboration and research opportunity.

AfriGenetry Link supports practical initiatives that connect science with communities, institutions and early-career researchers.

Problem / Approach / Audience / Outcome

Early-career research pathways

Problem: Young African researchers often lack structured opportunities to enter science ecosystems.

Approach: Provide learning, research exposure and mentorship pathways that support scientific confidence and skill building.

Audience: Students, early-career researchers and academic partners.

Outcome: A stronger pipeline of African scientists ready to contribute to health and genomics research.

Institutional research collaboration

Problem: Fragmented networks make it difficult to connect research expertise and resources across institutions.

Approach: Create pathways for institutional collaboration, knowledge exchange and joint project development.

Audience: Universities, research centers, health organizations and partners.

Outcome: Stronger research collaboration across the African science ecosystem.

Problem / Approach / Audience / Outcome

Genomics and health education

Problem: Scientific understanding is often too narrow or inaccessible for broader communities and learners.

Approach: Build learning spaces that connect genomic science, health and public engagement in clear language.

Audience: Students, professionals, communities and institutional stakeholders.

Outcome: Improved research literacy and more informed participation across disciplines.
